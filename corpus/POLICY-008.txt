**Effective Date: October 15, 2023**  

**Subject: Policy on Expense Claim Approval**  

To all employees of Meridian Analytics Ltd.,  

In alignment with our commitment to transparency and accountability in financial operations, we are implementing a new policy regarding the approval of expense claims. This policy is designed to streamline the claims process while ensuring that all expenses reflect our organization’s priorities and budgeting protocols.

Effective immediately, all expense claims must be submitted through the designated expense management system. Employees are required to complete the expense claim form, providing detailed descriptions, dates, and supporting documentation for all expenditures. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense to ensure timely approval and reimbursement. Late submissions may be subject to denial at the discretion of management, barring exceptional circumstances that must be documented and communicated to the approving authority.

All expense claims will be reviewed and approved by direct supervisors or managers. To ensure a smooth approval process, it is critical that the claims align with Meridian Analytics Ltd.'s business objectives and the specific guidelines outlined in our financial policy manual. Approval decisions should be communicated to the employee within five business days of submission. In cases where a claim is denied, the employee will be provided with a rationale for the decision, and they may appeal to the Chief Financial Officer within 15 days of the denial.

Training sessions will be organized by People Operations to familiarize all staff with the new system and procedures. These sessions will also cover best practices for submitting expense claims to minimize the likelihood of delays. Employees are encouraged to reach out to the People Operations department for further clarification or if they encounter any issues during the submission process.

We appreciate your cooperation and commitment to adhering to this policy, which aims to foster a culture of responsible financial management at Meridian Analytics Ltd. Thank you for your attention to this important matter.
