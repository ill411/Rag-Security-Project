**Financial Summary: Quarterly Departmental Budget Review - Q3 2024**  

As part of our ongoing efforts to ensure financial prudence and alignment with strategic goals, this report provides a detailed analysis of departmental budget allocations and expenditures for Q3 2024 at Meridian Analytics Ltd.

**Overview**  
For the third quarter of 2024, Meridian Analytics reported total revenues of $5.2 million, marking a steady increase of 12% compared to Q2 2024. This growth is attributed to enhanced customer engagement strategies and ongoing projects focusing on advanced analytics solutions. 

**Budget Allocations**  
In Q3 2024, the total departmental budget was set at $4.8 million, allocated as follows:

- **Research & Development (R&D)**: $1.8 million (37.5%)
- **Sales & Marketing**: $1.5 million (31.25%)
- **Operations**: $900,000 (18.75%)
- **Human Resources**: $450,000 (9.38%)
- **Finance & Administration**: $180,000 (3.75%)

**Departmental Performance**  

**1. Research & Development**  
The R&D department utilized $1.6 million of its budget, achieving a fantastic 88.89% utilization rate. Key projects, including the development of predictive analytics tools, are on track, with three prototypes expected for release by the end of Q4 2024.

**2. Sales & Marketing**  
Sales and marketing expenses totaled $1.4 million, which is a 93.33% budget utilization. The results from the recent digital campaign have shown a 25% increase in lead generation, significantly enhancing our sales pipeline.

**3. Operations**  
Operations spent $800,000, corresponding to an 88.89% utilization rate. Efficiency improvements have led to a reduction in operational costs by 10%, aligning closely with our cost-cutting objectives.

**4. Human Resources**  
The HR department has spent $400,000, achieving a 88.89% utilization rate. Investments in employee engagement and skills training are ongoing, aimed at improving workforce productivity.

**5. Finance & Administration**  
Finance and administration expenditures were limited to $150,000, at an 83.33% utilization rate. This reflects our increased focus on operational efficiency.

**Conclusion**  
Overall, Q3 2024 was a successful quarter for Meridian Analytics Ltd., with strategic spending and revenue growth positioning us well for continued success. As we move into Q4, monitoring of departmental budgets will remain crucial to ensure we meet our annual targets while continuing to innovate and expand our market presence.
