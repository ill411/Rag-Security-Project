**Meridian Analytics Ltd. Annual Revenue Summary - Fiscal Year 2025**

Meridian Analytics Ltd. has successfully concluded the third quarter of Fiscal Year 2025, with a robust revenue performance that highlights our strategic focus on data-driven solutions and advanced analytics services. Our total revenue for Q3 2025 reached $8.5 million, marking a 15% increase compared to $7.4 million in Q3 2024. Year-to-date, we have recorded a total revenue of $25.5 million, surpassing the $22 million benchmark set in the previous fiscal year.

The revenue growth can be attributed to several key factors, including the introduction of new AI-driven analytics tools, increased client engagement, and the expansion of our service offerings. In particular, our flagship product, the Meridian Insight Platform, has been well-received, generating $4 million in sales for Q3 alone, a 25% increase over the previous quarter. This product has become integral for enterprise clients looking to optimize their operations through data intelligence.

In terms of client acquisition, we onboarded a total of 12 new clients in the third quarter, contributing to a total client base growth of 30% year-on-year. This expansion is projected to bolster future revenue streams, with an estimated additional $2.5 million in projected revenue from these new partnerships over the next fiscal year.

Our budget allocations have been strategically adjusted to support this growth trajectory. The marketing and sales budget has been incremented by 20%, amounting to $1.5 million, to enhance outreach and client acquisition efforts. Additionally, R&D investment has been earmarked at $3 million to further innovate our product line and maintain our competitive edge.

However, operational expenses also saw an uptick, totaling $5 million for Q3, primarily due to increased staffing and infrastructure costs associated with our rapid growth. The overall operating margin stands at a healthy 41%, reflecting our ability to manage costs while scaling operations effectively.

Looking ahead, we remain optimistic about sustaining this revenue growth, with projected total revenues of approximately $35 million by the end of fiscal 2025. Our focus will continue to be on expanding our market share and enhancing client satisfaction through unparalleled data analytics insights.

**End of Summary**
