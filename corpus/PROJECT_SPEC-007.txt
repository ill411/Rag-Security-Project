**Project Specification: PROJ-Catalyst-006**  
**Project Title:** Client Services Workflow Optimization

**Objectives:**  
The primary goal of this project is to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of the Client Services team at Meridian Analytics Ltd. by developing an internal software tool that streamlines client onboarding, communication, and case management processes. Key objectives include:  
1. **Automating Workflows:** Create automated workflows to assist in onboarding new clients, ensuring that all necessary documentation is collected and processed efficiently.
2. **Centralized Communication:** Develop a communication hub that consolidates all client interactions, allowing team members to retrieve and log conversations, emails, and updates in one place.
3. **Data Analysis and Reporting:** Implement a reporting feature that analyzes client interaction data to identify trends, improving response times and customer satisfaction.
4. **User Training and Support:** Provide comprehensive training for the Client Services team to ensure seamless integration of the new tool into existing practices.

**Timeline:**  
The project will be executed over a period of six months, starting from January 2024 to June 2024. The timeline is broken down as follows:  
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4):** Requirements gathering and analysis – Engage with stakeholders to identify pain points and desired features.
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 5-12):** Development of the software prototype – Collaborate with developers to create a working model of the tool.
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 13-18):** Testing and feedback loop – Conduct user testing sessions and incorporate feedback for improvement.
- **Phase 4 (Weeks 19-24):** Final implementation and training – Roll out the finalized software, along with training sessions for the Client Services team.

**Budget Range:**  
The estimated budget for the project is between £50,000 and £70,000. This includes expenditures for software development, user testing, training resources, and operational overhead. A detailed budget will be outlined as each phase progresses and actual costs are better determined.

**Team Composition:**  
The project team will consist of the following members:  
- **Project Manager (1):** Oversee project execution, coordinate timelines, and manage communications between teams.  
- **Business Analyst (1):** Gather requirements and liaise with Client Services to identify needs.  
- **Software Developers (2):** Responsible for building and testing the software tool.  
- **UX/UI Designer (1):** Design the user interface, ensuring it is intuitive and user-friendly.  
- **Quality Assurance Specialist (1):** Conduct thorough testing to identify and resolve potential issues before deployment.  
- **Training Coordinator (1):** Create training materials and facilitate sessions for team members.

This project aims to empower the Client Services team, leading to improved client satisfaction and operational efficiencies.
