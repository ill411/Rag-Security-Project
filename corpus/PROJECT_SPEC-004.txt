**Project Specification: PROJ-Harbor-003**

**Project Title:** Enhancement of Client Interaction Management System (CIMS)

**Project Owner:** Client Services Team, Meridian Analytics Ltd.

**Project Objectives:**
The primary objective of the PROJ-Harbor-003 is to enhance the functionality and user experience of the Client Interaction Management System (CIMS). The enhancements will focus on improving data analytics capabilities, streamlining communication workflows, and integrating new client feedback mechanisms. Specifically, the goals are:
1. **Data Analytics Upgrades:** Implement advanced analytics tools that provide real-time insights into client interactions, enabling the team to proactively address client needs and optimize service delivery.
2. **Workflow Optimization:** Redesign the existing communication workflow within CIMS to reduce response times and enhance collaboration between teams when handling client inquiries.
3. **Feedback Integration:** Develop a user-friendly feedback collection feature that allows clients to easily provide input regarding their interactions, which will be analyzed to drive further improvements.

**Timeline:**
The project is expected to span a period of six months, with the following key milestones:
- **Phase 1: Requirements Gathering (Month 1):** Conduct workshops with stakeholders to detail system needs and client expectations.
- **Phase 2: Design and Planning (Month 2):** Develop a comprehensive design document outlining proposed changes and improvements.
- **Phase 3: Development (Months 3-4):** Execute the development of the new features and enhancements in an iterative Agile framework.
- **Phase 4: Testing (Month 5):** Conduct thorough testing phases, including unit, integration, and user acceptance testing, to ensure the system meets all functional requirements.
- **Phase 5: Deployment and Training (Month 6):** Roll out the updated CIMS to all client services personnel and provide necessary training sessions.

**Budget Range:**
The estimated budget for this project is between £100,000 and £150,000. This encompasses costs for software development, user training, and potential licensing fees for new analytics tools. A detailed budget breakdown will be created during the design phase to ensure transparency and accountability.

**Team Composition:**
The project will be executed by a cross-functional team composed of the following members:
- **Project Manager (1)**: Responsible for overall project oversight, timeline management, and stakeholder communication.
- **Business Analyst (2)**: To gather requirements and ensure that the needs of the client services team are captured accurately.
- **Software Developers (3)**: Tasked with building the new features and enhancements in the CIMS.
- **Quality Assurance Specialist (1)**: To ensure the integrity and functionality of the final product through rigorous testing.
- **Training Coordinator (1)**: To develop training materials and facilitate knowledge transfer to the client services team.

By adhering to this specification, Meridian Analytics Ltd. aims to substantially improve the efficiency and effectiveness of its client services, thereby enhancing client satisfaction and retention.
