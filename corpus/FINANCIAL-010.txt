**Financial Summary - Operating Cost Breakdown (Q2 2024)**

Meridian Analytics Ltd. continues to demonstrate prudent financial management as we review our operating costs for the second quarter of 2024. This report outlines the breakdown of our operating expenses and identifies areas for potential cost optimization.

**Total Operating Expenses**: $3,200,000

**1. Personnel Costs**: $1,800,000 (56.25%)  
Personnel costs, including salaries, benefits, and payroll taxes, represent the largest segment of our operating expenses. Notably, we increased headcount by 10% to support our expanding client base and product suite. This strategic investment is anticipated to enhance service delivery, yet we are exploring ways to streamline training and onboarding processes to control future personnel expenditures.

**2. Technology and Infrastructure**: $850,000 (26.56%)  
This category encompasses our IT maintenance, software licensing, and cloud service fees. Our shift to a subscription model for several software platforms has stabilized costs but requires ongoing scrutiny to ensure we are not over-committing resources. We are currently negotiating terms with our cloud provider to achieve a more favorable pricing structure as we scale.

**3. Marketing and Business Development**: $300,000 (9.37%)  
With ongoing efforts to strengthen our market presence, marketing expenses have increased by 15% over the previous quarter. The focus on digital campaigns has driven client engagement, but we are committed to assessing the ROI of these initiatives to ensure strategic allocation of marketing spend.

**4. Office and Administrative Expenses**: $150,000 (4.69%)  
This includes rent, utilities, supplies, and other overheads. We have implemented measures to reduce paper use and optimize office space, which contributed to a 5% decrease compared to Q1 2024. Further cost-saving initiatives are being discussed with senior management.

**5. Professional Fees**: $100,000 (3.13%)  
This expense covers consulting, legal, and audit services. While necessary for compliance and strategic guidance, we are conducting a review of our current contracts to identify potential for renegotiation or consolidation of services.

**Conclusion**  
The operating cost breakdown for Q2 2024 reflects our commitment to maintaining financial discipline while supporting growth initiatives. Moving forward, we aim to enhance operational efficiency through targeted reviews and cost control measures across all departments. Continuous monitoring and strategic adjustments will ensure that Meridian Analytics remains on a sustainable financial trajectory.
