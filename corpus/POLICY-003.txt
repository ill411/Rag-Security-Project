**Internal Memo**

**To:** All Employees  
**From:** Procurement Department  
**Date:** October 15, 2023  
**Subject:** Business Travel Policy  
**Reference Code:** POL-5129-002  

The purpose of this memo is to outline the Business Travel Policy for Meridian Analytics Ltd. to ensure effective management of employees' travel arrangements while maintaining compliance with company standards and budgetary constraints. This policy is effective immediately and applies to all employees who are required to travel for business purposes.

Employees are required to submit a travel request form at least two weeks prior to the intended travel date. This request must include the purpose of travel, proposed itinerary, estimated expenses, and any special requirements. The travel request will be evaluated based on necessity, projected expenses, and alignment with the company’s strategic objectives. Approval must be obtained from a direct supervisor as well as the Procurement Department, which will ensure all travel arrangements comply with budgetary guidelines.

Meridian Analytics Ltd. encourages employees to utilize approved vendors for travel bookings. Employees are expected to book travel through the designated travel management system to ensure compliance with corporate rates and alliances, which may provide savings opportunities. Any exceptions to booking outside of preferred vendors must be substantiated with a valid rationale and approved by the Procurement Department prior to booking. Travelers must strive to select the most economical options available while considering time constraints and personal safety.

In addition, all employees must adhere to the guidelines regarding travel expenses. Reimbursable expenses include, but are not limited to, transportation, accommodation, meals, and incidentals incurred solely for business purposes. Employees are required to retain all original receipts and submit an expense report within five business days of return from travel. Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in delayed reimbursements or denial of claims.

We appreciate your attention to the Business Travel Policy. Ensuring adherence to these guidelines will facilitate streamlined processes, maintain budgetary control, and support clear communication around travel arrangements. For any questions or clarifications regarding this policy, please reach out to the Procurement Department directly.

Thank you for your cooperation.
