### Project Overview
The goal of Project PROJ-Lumen-012 is to develop a streamlined Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system. The new CRM will integrate current customer data sources to enhance marketing efforts, improve customer engagement, and provide actionable insights leading to increased conversion rates. The marketing team has identified inefficiencies in tracking customer interactions and marketing campaigns, leading to missed opportunities for lead engagement.

### Objectives
1. **Data Integration**: Gather data from various sources including email marketing, social media, and website analytics to create a unified customer profile.
2. **Enhanced Reporting**: Develop automated reporting features to provide real-time insights into campaign performance and customer behavior.
3. **User-Friendly Interface**: Design an intuitive user interface that simplifies navigation for marketing personnel, reducing the learning curve associated with new software.
4. **Lead Scoring**: Implement a lead scoring system that utilizes machine learning to prioritize leads based on their likelihood to convert.

### Timeline
- **Phase 1: Requirement Gathering (Weeks 1-3)**  
  Conduct workshops with stakeholders to gather detailed requirements for the CRM system.
  
- **Phase 2: Design and Development (Weeks 4-10)**  
  Develop the system architecture and begin coding. Utilize Agile methodology for iterative development and ongoing feedback.

- **Phase 3: Testing (Weeks 11-13)**  
  Conduct unit testing followed by user acceptance testing (UAT) involving selected team members for feedback.

- **Phase 4: Deployment (Weeks 14-15)**  
  Launch the CRM system internally and provide training sessions for all relevant staff.

- **Phase 5: Review and Optimization (Weeks 16-18)**  
  Gather post-deployment feedback and optimize features based on user experience.

### Budget Range
The estimated budget for Project PROJ-Lumen-012 is between £50,000 and £75,000. This budget will cover software development costs, external consultancy (if needed), training materials, and any necessary software licenses.

### Team Composition
1. **Project Manager (1)**: Oversees project milestones, stakeholders' communication, and resource allocation.
2. **Business Analyst (1)**: Gathers requirements and translates business needs into technical specifications.
3. **Software Developers (2)**: Responsible for building the CRM system, both front-end and back-end development.
4. **UX/UI Designer (1)**: Designs a user-friendly interface that aligns with the company's branding.
5. **QA Tester (1)**: Focuses on testing the functionality and performance of the CRM before launch.
6. **Marketing Specialist (1)**: Provides insights into marketing strategies and how they can be integrated into the CRM.
