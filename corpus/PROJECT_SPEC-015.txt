**Project Specification: PROJ-Compass-014**  
**Project Title:** Enhancing Data Visualization Capabilities for Advanced Analytics

**Objective:**  
The objective of the PROJ-Compass-014 project is to develop and implement an advanced data visualization tool that leverages cutting-edge technologies to provide enhanced analytics capabilities at Meridian Analytics Ltd. The tool aims to streamline the data interpretation process for clients and internal stakeholders, enabling improved decision-making through interactive and intuitive visual representations of complex datasets. This project will focus on integrating machine learning algorithms to automatically generate insights and trends from real-time data, thereby increasing the efficiency of data analysis procedures.

**Timeline:**  
The project is expected to be completed within a six-month timeframe, beginning on January 15, 2024, and concluding by July 15, 2024. The timeline is broken down as follows:

- **Phase 1 (Research & Analysis) - January 15 to February 28, 2024:** Gather requirements, conduct user interviews, and analyze existing tools.
- **Phase 2 (Design) - March 1 to March 31, 2024:** Create wireframes and design prototypes for the visualization tool.
- **Phase 3 (Development) - April 1 to May 31, 2024:** Build the visualization tool, incorporating key features as identified in prior phases.
- **Phase 4 (Testing & Feedback) - June 1 to June 30, 2024:** Perform rigorous testing, gather user feedback, and make necessary adjustments.
- **Phase 5 (Deployment & Training) - July 1 to July 15, 2024:** Roll out the tool to all stakeholders and provide training sessions.

**Budget Range:**  
The expected budget for PROJ-Compass-014 is estimated to be between £50,000 to £70,000. This budget will cover software development costs, licensing fees for any third-party libraries or frameworks, personnel salaries, and expenses related to training and implementation. A detailed breakdown will be provided in subsequent proposals.

**Team Composition:**  
The project team will consist of the following roles: 

- **Project Manager (1)** - Responsible for overall project coordination and timeline adherence. 
- **Software Developers (3)** - Focus on the development of the visualization tool, including front-end and back-end functionalities.
- **UI/UX Designer (1)** - Tasked with creating user-friendly interfaces and ensuring an optimal user experience. 
- **Data Analyst (1)** - Provides expertise in data interpretation, working closely with developers to ensure the tool meets analytical needs.
- **Quality Assurance Tester (1)** - Responsible for testing the software and identifying bugs or areas for improvement before deployment.

This multi-disciplinary team is designed to leverage a blend of skills that will ensure the successful completion of PROJ-Compass-014, ultimately enhancing Meridian Analytics Ltd.'s capabilities in data visualization and analytics.
