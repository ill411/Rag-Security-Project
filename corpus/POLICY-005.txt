**Subject: Expense Claim Approval Policy**  

The purpose of this memo is to establish a formal policy regarding the approval process for employee expense claims at Meridian Analytics Ltd. This policy aims to streamline the expense reimbursement process, ensure compliance with internal controls, and promote transparency in financial transactions.

Effective immediately, all employee expense claims must be submitted through the designated expense reporting system, which will provide a standardized format for claim submissions. Employees are required to attach all relevant receipts and documentation in accordance with the types of allowable expenses outlined in the company’s Expense Policy document. Claims submitted without proper documentation will be returned to the employee for correction and resubmission. 

In terms of approval, all expense claims must be reviewed and authorized by the claimant’s direct supervisor. Supervisors are expected to evaluate each claim against the company's reimbursement guidelines to ensure that all expenses are legitimate and justifiable. Once approved, the supervisor will forward the claim to the finance department for processing. The finance department will conduct a final review to confirm that all claims adhere to Meridian Analytics Ltd.’s policies and that all necessary documentation is in order before issuing reimbursement.

Additionally, employees are reminded that any claims exceeding the threshold of $500 must receive additional authorization from the Head of the respective department prior to submission. This additional layer of approval is intended to mitigate risks associated with higher-value claims and to ensure fiscal responsibility. 

Training sessions on the expense claim submission process will be held quarterly, and employees are encouraged to reach out to the finance department for any queries concerning claim approvals or the associated procedures. It is crucial that all staff members familiarize themselves with this policy and adhere to these guidelines to maintain financial integrity within the organization.
