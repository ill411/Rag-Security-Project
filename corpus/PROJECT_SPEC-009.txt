**Project Specification: PROJ-Summit-008**  
**Project Title:** EffiencyBoost: Marketing Workflow Automation  

**Project Objectives:**
The primary aim of the EffiencyBoost project is to enhance operational efficiency within the Marketing team at Meridian Analytics Ltd. By developing and implementing an advanced workflow automation software, we seek to streamline repetitive tasks, improve campaign management, and boost overall team productivity. Key objectives include:

1. **Automating Routine Tasks:** Minimize time spent on repetitive tasks such as data entry, report generation, and social media scheduling.
2. **Centralized Campaign Management:** Create a unified platform for managing marketing campaigns, allowing for better tracking of performance metrics and ensuring timely execution.
3. **Enhanced Collaboration:** Foster greater collaboration among team members through integrated communication tools and project tracking features.
4. **Scalability:** Design the system to be scalable, allowing for easy adaptation as the company grows or as marketing needs evolve.

**Timeline:**
The project is expected to span six months, divided into the following phases:

- **Phase 1: Requirement Gathering (Weeks 1-4)** – Engage with key stakeholders to gather requirements and define user stories.
- **Phase 2: Development (Weeks 5-16)** – The software will be designed and coded, with regular sprints to ensure flexibility and iterative feedback.
- **Phase 3: Testing (Weeks 17-20)** – Conduct thorough testing, including user acceptance testing (UAT) to ensure functionality meets team expectations.
- **Phase 4: Deployment (Weeks 21-24)** – Implement the software across the Marketing team, including training sessions and support resources.

**Budget Range:**
The estimated budget for EffiencyBoost is set within the range of $75,000 to $100,000. This budget will cover:

- **Software Development Costs:** Salary compensation for developers and essential software tools.
- **Testing & Quality Assurance:** Resources allocated to ensure a robust testing phase.
- **Training & Support:** Costs associated with training sessions for the Marketing team and ongoing support.

**Team Composition:**
The project team will consist of the following members to ensure a well-rounded approach:

- **Project Manager (1):** Oversees the project timeline, budget, and stakeholder communication.
- **Business Analyst (1):** Gathers requirements and translates them into technical specifications.
- **Software Developers (2):** Responsible for building the workflow automation software.
- **UI/UX Designer (1):** Works on user interface design for optimal user experience.
- **Quality Assurance Specialist (1):** Ensures the software meets defined requirements and is free of critical bugs.
- **Marketing Team Representatives (2):** Provide insights and feedback from an end-user perspective throughout the project.

This comprehensive project specification aims to align the Marketing team’s operational goals with innovative technological solutions, enhancing efficacy and collaboration within Meridian Analytics Ltd.
