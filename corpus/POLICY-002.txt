**Memo**

**To:** All Employees  
**From:** People Operations Department  
**Date:** October 10, 2023  
**Subject:** Leave Entitlement Policy  
**Policy Reference Number:** POL-3407-001  

---

The People Operations Department is pleased to announce the implementation of the Leave Entitlement Policy, effective October 15, 2023. This policy is designed to clarify and streamline the processes surrounding the various types of leave available to employees at Meridian Analytics Ltd. We aim to promote a healthy work-life balance while ensuring that all employees understand their entitlements and responsibilities related to leave.

Under this policy, employees are entitled to various forms of leave, including but not limited to annual leave, sick leave, maternity/paternity leave, and unpaid leave. All full-time employees shall accrue annual leave at a rate of 20 days per year, which will be prorated for part-time employees based on their hours worked. Sick leave is accrued at a rate of 10 days per calendar year, with unused sick days rolling over to the next year, up to a maximum of 50 days. Maternity and paternity leave will be provided in accordance with statutory requirements and company guidelines, ensuring that employees can take the necessary time to care for their families.

Employees must submit leave requests in writing to their direct supervisors at least two weeks in advance for annual leave and as soon as reasonably possible for sick leave or other emergency leave needs. All requests will be reviewed by management, taking into consideration project timelines and team coverage to ensure that operational responsibilities are met. Should an employee require leave that exceeds the standard entitlement, a formal request for unpaid leave must be submitted for consideration, which will be evaluated based on individual circumstances and the needs of the team.

Failure to comply with the leave request procedures may result in denial of leave. The People Operations Department encourages all employees to familiarize themselves with this policy and to reach out should they have any questions or require clarification regarding their leave entitlements. We believe that this policy will contribute positively to employee well-being and productivity, and we appreciate your cooperation in adhering to these guidelines. 

For further details, please refer to the employee handbook or contact the People Operations Department directly. Thank you for your attention to this important policy. 

--- 

**End of Memo**
