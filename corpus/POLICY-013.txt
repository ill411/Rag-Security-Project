**Issued by: Legal Department**  
**Effective Date: January 1, 2024**  

**Subject: Remote Work Eligibility**

In response to the evolving work landscape and the continued emphasis on flexibility and employee satisfaction, Meridian Analytics Ltd. is establishing clear guidelines regarding remote work eligibility. This policy is designed to maintain productivity, ensure compliance with legal standards, and foster a collaborative work environment, whether in-office or remote.

Eligibility for remote work will generally be extended to employees whose roles permit flexibility without significantly impacting team operations or customer interactions. This includes positions that primarily involve independent tasks, data analysis, or digital communications. It is essential that employees demonstrate a consistent record of performance and reliability. Remote work options will be assessed on a case-by-case basis, with direct supervisors and department heads responsible for making determinations based on the specific job functions and departmental needs.

Employees seeking to work remotely must submit a formal request to their supervisor, outlining their rationale for the arrangement. Factors to be considered include the nature of the job, previous performance reviews, potential impacts on team dynamics, and opportunities for collaboration. Supervisors will review requests in alignment with departmental objectives and productivity metrics. Approved arrangements will be outlined in a Remote Work Agreement, specifying expectations regarding availability, communication, and deliverables.

Meridian Analytics Ltd. remains committed to promoting a work culture that supports both employee well-being and organizational effectiveness. To that end, departments will periodically evaluate remote work policies to adapt to ongoing changes in business needs and employee feedback. Regular reviews will ensure that remote work remains conducive to achieving the company’s goals while supporting a healthy work-life balance for all employees.

For any questions or clarifications regarding this policy, please contact the Legal Department. Thank you for your cooperation and support in implementing this important initiative.
