**Report ID: FIN-2025-Q3-003**  
**Company: Meridian Analytics Ltd.**  
**Date: September 30, 2025**  

**Annual Revenue Summary**  

Meridian Analytics Ltd. is pleased to present its annual revenue summary for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2025. This report outlines our financial performance, key revenue streams, and budget allocations that demonstrate a solid growth trajectory and strategic investments in our core business areas.

For the fiscal year 2025, Meridian Analytics recorded a total revenue of $15.3 million, representing a 20% increase compared to the previous year’s revenue of $12.8 million. This growth can be attributed to our strategic expansion into new markets and the successful launch of two innovative software products aimed at enhancing data analytics capabilities for our clients.

Our revenue breakdown is as follows:

- **Software Licensing**: $8.2 million (53.5% of total revenue)  
  Software licensing revenue saw a 25% increase year-over-year due to an uptick in demand from enterprises seeking advanced data solutions.  

- **Consulting Services**: $5.1 million (33.3% of total revenue)  
  Revenue from consulting services also rose by 15%, reflecting our commitment to providing exceptional support and expertise to our clients.  

- **Training and Support Services**: $1.5 million (9.8% of total revenue)  
  This category grew by 10%, driven by our focus on ensuring clients maximize their use of our products.  

- **Partnership Revenues**: $0.5 million (3.4% of total revenue)  
  Revenues from strategic partnerships remained steady as we solidified collaborations with key industry players.

In terms of budget allocation, for fiscal year 2025, Meridian Analytics invested $4.5 million in Research and Development, accounting for 29.4% of our total revenue. This investment is aimed at enhancing our product offerings and ensuring we stay ahead of industry trends. Additionally, $2 million was allocated to marketing efforts (13.1% of total revenue) to bolster brand presence and drive customer engagement. Administrative and operational costs accounted for $3 million, while remaining funds were reserved for potential acquisition opportunities to accelerate growth.

In summary, Meridian Analytics Ltd. has demonstrated a robust financial performance for fiscal year 2025. Our strategic investments and focus on market expansion position us well for continued growth in the coming years. We remain committed to delivering value to our stakeholders while enhancing our capabilities in the analytics space.
