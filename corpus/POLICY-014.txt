**Subject: Confidentiality Obligations**

To all Procurement Department personnel,

In line with Meridian Analytics Ltd.'s commitment to protecting sensitive information and maintaining the highest standards of integrity, this memorandum outlines the confidentiality obligations that each employee within the Procurement Department must adhere to during and after their employment. These obligations are critical to safeguarding our proprietary data, business strategies, supplier relationships, and client information.

All employees are required to treat any information related to the company’s procurement processes, including pricing, contracts, supplier communications, and internal discussions, as strictly confidential. Disclosures of such information, whether intentional or accidental, could lead to significant implications for our competitive advantage and reputation. As part of your responsibilities, you must ensure that any documents, electronic communications, and discussions that pertain to procurement operations are securely stored and only shared with authorized personnel who have a legitimate need to know. 

Upon termination of employment, or at any time upon request, employees are required to return all company property, including documents and electronic files related to procurement processes. This encompasses both physical copies and digital data stored on personal devices or accounts. Furthermore, employees are reminded that confidentiality obligations remain in effect even after leaving the company; any breach of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including legal proceedings.

To support compliance with these obligations, regular training sessions will be conducted, focusing on data protection best practices and the importance of confidentiality. Employees are encouraged to reach out to their supervisor or the Compliance Officer if they have any questions or require clarification regarding this policy. Your adherence to these confidentiality obligations is essential to maintaining the trust of our suppliers and clients, as well as the integrity of Meridian Analytics Ltd.

Thank you for your cooperation and diligence in upholding our commitment to confidentiality.
