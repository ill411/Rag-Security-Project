**Overview**  
Meridian Analytics Ltd. has concluded another fruitful fiscal year, marking significant progress across various sectors of our business. The following summary encapsulates our annual revenue performance, providing insights into key revenue streams and budget allocations for the year ending March 31, 2025.

**Annual Revenue Summary**  
For the fiscal year 2024/2025, Meridian Analytics Ltd. reported a total revenue of **$12.3 million**, reflecting an increase of **15%** compared to the previous year's revenue of **$10.7 million**. This growth can be attributed to the expansion of our product offerings, increased client acquisitions, and effective marketing strategies executed throughout the year.

**Revenue Breakdown**  
The revenue growth was primarily driven by the following segments:

1. **Software Solutions**: This sector generated **$7.5 million**, accounting for **61%** of our total revenue. The increase from last year's **$6.0 million** can be linked directly to the launch of our new data analytics platform.

2. **Consulting Services**: Revenue from consulting services increased to **$3.0 million** from **$2.5 million**, representing a **20%** rise. Our reputation for providing high-quality advisory services has facilitated deeper relationships with current clients and attracted new ones.

3. **Training and Support**: We saw a revenue increase from **$1.2 million** to **$1.8 million** in this area, marking a **50%** growth. This reflects an upward trend in demand for training services as businesses increasingly recognize the importance of data literacy.

**Budget Allocations**  
In line with our revenue growth strategy, Meridian Analytics Ltd. has allocated **$8.0 million** for operational expenses. Key allocations include:

- **Research and Development**: **$2.5 million** (31% of total budget) to enhance our existing products and develop new solutions.
  
- **Marketing and Sales**: **$2.0 million** (25% of total budget) to fortify our market presence and expand our client base.

- **General and Administrative Expenses**: **$3.5 million** (44% of total budget) to support our expanding workforce and improve operational efficiencies.

**Outlook**  
Looking ahead, Meridian Analytics Ltd. aims to sustain this momentum by focusing on innovative solutions and strategic partnerships. We are projected to reach a revenue of **$15 million** in the upcoming fiscal year by continuing to leverage our strong market position and investing in emerging technologies.
