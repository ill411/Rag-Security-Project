**Project Specification: PROJ-Summit-002**

**Project Title:** Enhancement of Data Extraction Processes for Real-Time Analytics

**Company:** Meridian Analytics Ltd.

**Project Owner:** Engineering Team

**Objectives:**

The primary objective of the PROJ-Summit-002 initiative is to enhance and optimize the existing data extraction processes to facilitate real-time analytics capabilities across the organization. This improvement aims to reduce the data latency from our sources to the analytics dashboard by at least 50%, ensuring that all stakeholders can make informed decisions based on the latest data. 

Specific objectives include:

1. **Integration of Advanced ETL Tools:** Evaluate and implement advanced Extract, Transform, Load (ETL) tools that support real-time data processing capabilities.
  
2. **Improvement of Data Pipeline Efficiency:** Redesign the current data pipeline architecture to support asynchronous data fetching, which will enhance efficiency and reliability.

3. **Implementation of Automated Monitoring Systems:** Establish automated monitoring systems to track data flow and alert teams to potential issues in real-time, ensuring minimal downtime.

4. **Training and Documentation:** Provide comprehensive training for engineering and analytics teams on the new processes and tools, along with detailed documentation for ongoing reference.

**Timeline:**

The project will be executed over a six-month period, broken down into the following phases:

- **Phase 1 (Month 1):** Requirements gathering and initial research on potential ETL tools and technologies.
  
- **Phase 2 (Months 2-3):** Selection and procurement of ETL tools, followed by redesigning the data architecture.

- **Phase 3 (Month 4):** Development and integration of the improved data pipeline.

- **Phase 4 (Month 5):** Implementation of monitoring systems and training sessions for relevant teams.

- **Phase 5 (Month 6):** Final testing, user feedback collection, and project closure.

**Budget Range:**

The projected budget for the PROJ-Summit-002 initiative is estimated to be between £120,000 and £150,000. This budget encompasses costs related to software licensing, training sessions, and personnel (including overtime for engineering team members) and any required infrastructure upgrades.

**Team Composition:**

The project will be staffed with a cross-functional team including:

- **Project Manager (1):** Responsible for overseeing the project timeline and deliverables.

- **Data Engineers (2):** To focus on the technical aspects of ETL tool integration and architecture redesign.

- **Data Scientists (2):** To provide input on data requirements and ensure analytics needs are met.

- **Quality Assurance Specialists (1):** To ensure robust testing and validation of the new pipeline.

- **Technical Writer (1):** To document the new processes and create training materials for the team.

This carefully chosen team composition will ensure that all aspects of the project are effectively addressed, paving the way for successful implementation and long-term improvements in data analytics capabilities at Meridian Analytics Ltd.
