**Financial Summary Number 12: Headcount and Payroll Allocation for Meridian Analytics Ltd.**

As of Q4 2024, Meridian Analytics Ltd. reports a total headcount of 250 employees, reflecting a 15% increase from the previous quarter. This rise in personnel is primarily attributed to the expansion of our data analytics and cybersecurity divisions, thereby enhancing our service capabilities in response to increased market demand.

**Payroll Allocation Overview:**
The major components of our payroll allocation include salaries, benefits, and bonuses, totaling an annual expenditure of $15 million for the current fiscal year. The payroll budget has been meticulously structured, ensuring each department is adequately funded to meet operational needs while remaining within financial constraints.

1. **Salaries:** The total salary expenditure accounts for approximately $11 million, with the average annual salary per employee standing at $44,000. The increase in headcount resulted in an additional salary expense of $1.5 million compared to Q3 2024, reflective of the hiring of specialized roles such as data scientists, cybersecurity analysts, and AI developers.

2. **Benefits:** Employee benefits, comprising health insurance, retirement contributions, and other perks, represent 20% of total payroll expenses, amounting to $3 million. Noteworthy updates to our benefit packages this quarter included enhancements to our health plans and the introduction of a wellness program to foster employee engagement and productivity.

3. **Bonuses:** The bonus structure for performance-based incentives allocated $1 million for the current fiscal year. This figure includes both annual performance bonuses and quarterly recognitions, encouraging employees to meet or exceed their targets. A significant portion of this bonus allocation will be distributed at the end of Q4, rewarding the exceptional performance recorded during our peak operational periods.

4. **Training and Development:** Our commitment to professional development has prompted an allocation of $500,000 for employee training initiatives in Q4. This investment focuses on upskilling our workforce, particularly in emerging technologies relevant to data analytics and cybersecurity.

In summary, Meridian Analytics Ltd. is actively fostering an environment of growth and opportunity through strategic headcount expansion and well-structured payroll allocation. This strategic approach not only enhances employee satisfaction but also positions the company for sustained competitive advantage in the data analytics and cybersecurity markets. 

**End of Summary**
