**Project Specification: PROJ-Atlas-004**  
**Project Title:** Employee Engagement Enhancement Tool  
**Project Owner:** People Operations Team  
**Date:** October 6, 2023

**Objectives:**  
The primary objective of the Employee Engagement Enhancement Tool (EEET) is to develop a comprehensive digital platform that facilitates real-time feedback and engagement tracking. The tool will allow employees to share their experiences and opinions, fostering a culture of open communication. Key objectives include:

1. **Feedback Mechanism:** Create a user-friendly interface for employees to provide anonymous feedback about workplace policies, culture, and management.
2. **Engagement Metrics:** Develop a dashboard that captures and analyzes engagement metrics on a monthly basis, providing actionable insights for team leaders and upper management.
3. **Action Tracking:** Enable the HR team to identify trends in feedback and measure the effectiveness of implemented changes through follow-up surveys.
4. **Integration with Existing Systems:** Ensure compatibility with current HR systems and tools to streamline data collection and reporting.

**Timeline:**  
The project is expected to unfold over a period of 6 months, structured as follows:

- **Phase 1: Requirement Gathering (Month 1)**  
  Engage with diverse teams across the organization to collect insights on desired features and functionalities.

- **Phase 2: Design and Development (Months 2-4)**  
  Develop the initial version of the EEET, including all key functionalities and user interfaces, followed by user acceptance testing.

- **Phase 3: Implementation and Training (Months 5-6)**  
  Roll out the tool organization-wide, accompanied by training sessions for all employees to ensure proper utilization and to gather initial feedback for adjustments.

**Budget Range:**  
The projected budget for the Employee Engagement Enhancement Tool is estimated to be between $75,000 and $100,000. This includes costs for software development, testing, training, and ongoing maintenance for the first year. Specific budget allocations will include:

- $50,000 for software development and implementation.
- $15,000 for user training and support.
- $10,000 for data analysis tools and integration.
- $5,000 for marketing materials to promote the tool within the organization.

**Team Composition:**  
To execute this project effectively, the following roles will be essential:

- **Project Manager (1)**: Overall project oversight, coordination with stakeholders.
- **Business Analyst (1)**: Requirement gathering and documentation.
- **Software Developers (2)**: Design and development of the tool.
- **UX/UI Designer (1)**: Enhancing user experience and interface design.
- **HR Representative (1)**: Liaison for employee needs and feedback integration.
- **Data Analyst (1)**: Tracking engagement metrics and providing insights.

The successful implementation of the EEET will promote a more engaging workplace culture at Meridian Analytics Ltd., ultimately leading to enhanced employee satisfaction and retention.
