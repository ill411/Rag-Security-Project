### Operating Cost Breakdown for Q3 2023

This summary provides an insight into the operating costs incurred by Meridian Analytics Ltd. during the third quarter of 2023, highlighting significant expenditure areas and assessing their alignment with our strategic objectives.

In Q3 2023, the total operating costs amounted to **$2.5 million**, reflecting a **12% increase** from the previous quarter. This increase can primarily be attributed to the enhanced investment in technology and workforce development aimed at ensuring sustained growth and competitive advantage.

**Operating Cost Allocation:**

1. **Personnel Costs: $1.2 million (48%)**
   - Employee salaries and wages accounted for **$900,000**, up from **$800,000** in Q2 due to new hires in the data analytics and software development teams.
   - Employee benefits, including health insurance and retirement contributions, totaled **$300,000**. This also included bonus payments affecting our cost structure as performance metrics were exceeded.

2. **Technology and Infrastructure: $800,000 (32%)**
   - The IT infrastructure costs, including software licenses and hardware upgrades, reached **$500,000**. This investment was crucial for enhancing our analytical capacities and ensuring the security of client data.
   - Cloud service expenses, critical for our data storage and processing capabilities, accounted for **$300,000**. As we have expanded our client base, these costs are projected to increase further in Q4.

3. **Marketing and Client Acquisition: $300,000 (12%)**
   - Marketing expenditures aimed at increasing brand awareness and client acquisition included digital marketing campaigns and industry conference participation. This was a **15% increase** compared to Q2, reflecting our strategic push for growth in new market segments.

4. **Operational Expenses: $200,000 (8%)**
   - Miscellaneous operational costs, such as administrative expenses and utilities, decreased from **$250,000**, attributed to cost-saving measures implemented across departments.

### Summary and Outlook

The increase in operating costs is closely tied to our strategic initiatives to enhance our technological capabilities and expand our workforce. In Q4, we anticipate these costs will continue to rise as we roll out additional marketing campaigns and invest further in technology to support our growing client base.

Maintaining a close watch on these expenditures will be essential as we aim to balance growth with profitability. The operational efficiency measures planned for Q4 will be pivotal in managing cost increases while pursuing our long-term objectives.
