Subject: Leave Entitlement Policy  

The purpose of this memo is to outline and clarify the leave entitlement policy for all employees of Meridian Analytics Ltd. This policy is designed to ensure that all employees are aware of their rights and the procedures necessary to request and utilize leave effectively.

As per company guidelines, all full-time employees are entitled to a total of 20 days of paid annual leave, accruing at the rate of 1.67 days per month of continuous employment. Part-time employees will receive leave entitlements proportionate to their work hours. It is essential for employees to be aware that these leave days are provided to promote work-life balance and employee well-being. Any leave days not utilized by the end of the calendar year will not carry over into the subsequent year unless expressly approved by management.

Employees must submit leave requests at least two weeks in advance, except in cases of emergency or unforeseen circumstances. Requests should be addressed to the immediate supervisor and should include the intended duration and purpose of the leave. Supervisors retain the authority to approve or deny leave requests based on operational requirements, staffing levels, and overall workload. It is the responsibility of each employee to manage their leave entitlements proactively and to ensure that they plan leave in such a manner that does not disrupt workflow.

For exceptional circumstances such as parental leave, medical leave, or sabbaticals that exceed the standard leave entitlements, employees are required to consult with the HR department. Specific provisions and guidelines are detailed in the Employee Handbook, which is available electronically on the company portal. We encourage all employees to familiarize themselves with these guidelines to ensure a clear understanding of their rights and obligations concerning leave entitlements.

Should you have any questions related to this policy or need further clarification, please do not hesitate to reach out to the Finance Department or the HR team for assistance.
