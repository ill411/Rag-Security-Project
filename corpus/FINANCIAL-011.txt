**Report ID: FIN-2025-Q1-010**  
**Financial Summary Number 11: Operating Cost Breakdown**  
**Meridian Analytics Ltd.**  
**Date: April 30, 2025**

Meridian Analytics Ltd. has completed its financial analysis for the first quarter of 2025, focusing on the breakdown of operating costs that directly impact our profitability and overall financial health. Understanding these costs is crucial as we strive to optimize our budget allocations and increase operational efficiency.

**Total Operating Costs: $2,500,000**  
Meridian Analytics Ltd. has recorded total operating costs of $2,500,000 for Q1 2025, reflecting a stable increase of 10% from Q4 2024. The primary components of these costs are categorized as follows:

1. **Personnel Expenses: $1,200,000**  
   Personnel expenses constitute the largest portion of our operating costs, accounting for 48% of the total. This figure includes salaries, benefits, and payroll taxes for our 150 employees. The increase in personnel costs can be attributed to a 5% salary raise implemented in January 2025 to retain talent and align with market standards.

2. **Technology and Software Licensing: $600,000**  
   As a data-driven company, investing in technology remains critical. In Q1 2025, we spent $600,000 on technology infrastructure, which includes software licenses, cloud services, and hardware upgrades. This expense represents 24% of our total operating costs and is essential for maintaining our competitive edge in data analytics.

3. **Marketing and Sales: $400,000**  
   Marketing and sales expenses totaled $400,000, making up 16% of our operating costs. This allocation reflects our ongoing efforts to penetrate new markets and develop brand awareness through digital marketing campaigns and sales initiatives. A substantial portion of this cost has been dedicated to lead generation and customer engagement strategies.

4. **Office and Administrative Expenses: $300,000**  
   Office and administrative expenses accounted for 12% of our operating costs, totaling $300,000. This includes rent, utilities, office supplies, and general administrative overhead. While this cost remains relatively stable, we have identified areas for potential savings in future quarters.

5. **Research and Development: $50,000**  
   While R&D spending is relatively low at $50,000, this represents a commitment to innovation and the development of new analytical tools. The allocation equates to 2% of total operating costs and highlights our focus on maintaining a forward-thinking approach.

In summary, our operating costs reflect our strategic priorities, with a significant focus on personnel and technology investments. Going forward, we aim to optimize these costs while enhancing operational efficiency to ensure continued profitability and growth at Meridian Analytics Ltd.
