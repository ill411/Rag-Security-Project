**SUBJECT: Workplace Conduct Policy**

To ensure a productive and respectful work environment at Meridian Analytics Ltd., it is essential that all employees understand the expectations regarding workplace conduct. This memorandum outlines the key principles and guidelines that govern behavior in the workplace, effective immediately. 

All employees are expected to uphold a standard of professionalism in their interactions with colleagues, clients, and stakeholders. Disrespectful behavior, including but not limited to bullying, harassment, or discrimination based on race, gender, age, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic, will not be tolerated. The Finance Department encourages open communication and collaboration while maintaining an atmosphere of respect and integrity. Employees are urged to report any incidents of workplace misconduct to their immediate supervisor or the Human Resources department without fear of retaliation.

Additionally, it is imperative that all employees demonstrate accountability for their actions. This includes arriving on time for scheduled work hours, meeting deadlines, and adhering to company policies and procedures. Employees are encouraged to seek clarification or guidance when necessary and should engage in constructive feedback when interacting with peers. Teamwork is a core value of Meridian Analytics Ltd., and working collaboratively is vital for the success of our finance operations.

Finally, to promote a healthy work-life balance, employees are encouraged to manage their time effectively and utilize breaks and time-off as per company policy. All employees should familiarize themselves with the company’s Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for support in personal or professional matters that may impact their workplace conduct. As we strive for excellence at Meridian Analytics Ltd., it is our collective responsibility to foster a positive working environment and uphold the values of our organization.

Thank you for your attention to this important matter and for your continued commitment to maintaining a professional and respectful workplace.
