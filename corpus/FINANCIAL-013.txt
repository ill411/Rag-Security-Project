**Headcount and Payroll Allocation Overview**

As of the end of Q1 2023, Meridian Analytics Ltd. has a total workforce of 150 employees, reflecting an increase of 10% compared to Q4 2022. This growth aligns with our strategic goals to enhance our analytical capabilities and expand service offerings in the data analytics sector. The headcount distribution is as follows:

- **Data Analysts**: 60  
- **Data Scientists**: 30  
- **Software Developers**: 25  
- **Sales and Marketing**: 20  
- **Administrative Staff**: 15  

**Payroll Allocation**

For Q1 2023, the total payroll expenditure reached $2.5 million, accounting for approximately 40% of our total operating expenses. The payroll structure is detailed below:

- **Salaries**: $2 million (80%)  
- **Employee Benefits**: $400,000 (16%)  
- **Bonuses and Performance Incentives**: $100,000 (4%)  

The allocation emphasizes our commitment to competitive compensation as a means to attract and retain top talent within the industry. Salaries across all departments were adjusted by an average of 5% to accommodate inflation and adhere to market standards. 

**Budget Highlights**

Our payroll budget for 2023 is set at $10 million, which includes provisions for anticipated increases in headcount, competitive salary adjustments, and enhanced benefits offerings. The breakdown of the budget is as follows:

- **Data Analysts**: $4 million  
- **Data Scientists**: $3 million  
- **Software Developers**: $2 million  
- **Sales and Marketing**: $1 million  

To foster employee satisfaction and productivity, we have introduced new benefits such as remote work stipends, wellness programs, and professional development allowances, which represent an additional $1 million allocation in employee benefits for the year.

**Conclusion**

Meridian Analytics Ltd. remains focused on strategic investments in human capital, believing that our employees are our strongest asset. The headcount increase and thoughtful payroll allocation are designed to ensure that we not only maintain a competitive edge in the data analytics market but also foster a healthy and rewarding work environment. Continuous monitoring and adjustments will be made throughout the year to respond adequately to market dynamics and internal growth.
